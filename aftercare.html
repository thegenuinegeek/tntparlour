Healing & Care

Tattoo Aftercare Guide.

Proper healing keeps lines crisp and color saturated for decades. Always prioritize any custom care instructions your artist gives you before leaving 484 Cherry St.

Second-Skin Film

Routine 01: Soku Second-Skin Wrap

Follow these six steps if your artist sealed your fresh tattoo with Soku Tattoo Aftercare film.

  1. Keep the wrap on for 3 to 7 daysLeave Soku Tattoo Aftercare on the tattoo for at least 3 days. You may leave the protective film on up to a full week as long as the seal remains intact and the skin is not irritated.
  2. Expect dark fluid underneathDuring the first few days, plasma, blood, and excess surface ink will pool as a dark, cloudy liquid inside the pocket. This is completely normal and protects the healing tattoo.
  3. Remove gently in a warm showerAfter at least 3 days, peel the wrap off slowly in the shower using warm water and Dial Gold antibacterial soap. Lift a corner carefully until soap and water break the adhesive seal without pulling on the skin.
  4. Wash morning and night for 2 daysFor the next two days after removing the film, gently wash the tattoo with Dial Gold morning and night. Pat completely dry with a clean disposable paper towel—never rub with a cloth bath towel.
  5. Moisturize lightly starting day 3On the third day after film removal, apply a thin layer of unscented, dye-free lotion if the skin feels dry or tight. Shop-recommended options include Lubriderm, Jergens, Cornhuskers, Curel, or Eucerin.
  6. Remove early if the seal leaksIf fluid leaks out from an edge before day 3, bacteria can get in. Remove the wrap immediately in the shower and transition straight to the traditional wash-and-lotion routine below.

Standard Bandage

Routine 02: Standard Bandage & Ointment

Follow these eight steps if your artist sent you home with a traditional wrap or absorbent pad.

  1. First wash when you get homeLeave the shop bandage on until you arrive home. Remove it cleanly, wash gently with Dial Gold antibacterial soap, and pat dry with a clean paper towel. Wash once more at bedtime; if you have pets, you may cover it loosely with clean cling wrap on the first night only once completely dry.
  2. Wash twice daily for days 1 to 4For the first 3 to 4 days, wash your tattoo with Dial Gold morning and night—and any time you sweat heavily or get dirty—then pat dry with a fresh paper towel.
  3. Apply a whisper-thin layer of AquaphorDuring days 1 to 4, rub in a very thin sheen of Aquaphor or an artist-approved ointment. Applying too much ointment traps moisture, suffocates pores, and causes heavy scabbing or weeping.
  4. Switch to unscented lotion around day 5Around day 5, swap ointment for a small amount of fragrance-free, dye-free lotion (Lubriderm, Jergens, Cornhuskers, Curel, or Eucerin) as needed for about two weeks until peeling settles.
  5. Know what normal healing feels likeMild redness, warmth, itching, light flaking, and slight swelling are normal parts of the healing cycle. Never scratch or pick peeling flakes—pat gently or apply a light dot of lotion.
  6. Keep scented soaps off in the showerQuick showers are fine, but keep harsh body washes, scrubs, and shampoo away from the fresh tattoo. If soap runs over it, rinse and wash gently with Dial Gold at the end of your shower.
  7. No soaking or swimming for 2 to 3 weeksAvoid pools, lakes, oceans, hot tubs, and soaking in bathtubs for at least 2 to 3 weeks until the surface skin is fully healed.
  8. Protect fresh ink from direct sunKeep a healing tattoo covered from direct sunlight and tanning beds. Once fully healed, daily broad-spectrum sunscreen keeps lines sharp and colors bright for years.

Questions During Healing

Unsure How Something Is Healing?
