Healing

Tattoo aftercare.

Confirm with your artist These steps started from the shop's previous website. Products and timing may have changed. Ask before you leave the chair, and follow what your artist tells you if it differs from this page.

If you were wrapped with Soku Tattoo Aftercare

  1. Leave Soku Tattoo Aftercare on the tattoo for at least 3 days. You can leave the wrap on for up to a week if it isn't irritating you.
  2. During those days the tattoo will make a dark, cloudy liquid under the bandage. That's blood, plasma, and extra ink. It's normal, and it helps the piece heal.
  3. After at least 3 days, take the bandage off slowly in the shower with Dial antibacterial soap (Dial Gold). The wrap usually sticks only to the skin around the tattoo. Pick a corner until you reach the pocket over the tattoo, and let soap and water get under it until it comes off comfortably.
  4. For the next 2 days after the bandage is off, wash with Dial Gold morning and night. Pat dry with a paper towel. Don't rub it with a cloth towel.
  5. On the 3rd day after you remove the bandage, use a small amount of unscented, dye-free lotion if it's dry or itchy. The previous instructions named Lubriderm, Jergens, Cornhuskers, Curel, or Eucerin.
  6. If the liquid leaks before those first 3 days, take the bandage off the same way and switch to the steps below.

If you were not bandaged with Soku

  1. Leave the bandage on until you get home. Then take it off, wash lightly with Dial Gold, and pat dry with a clean paper towel. You don't need to do anything else until bed. At bedtime, wash with Dial Gold again and pat dry. If you have pets, cover the tattoo with cling wrap for the first night only, after it's as dry as you can get it.
  2. For the first 3–4 days, wash with Dial Gold morning and night, and again any time you get sweaty or dirty. Pat dry with a paper towel.
  3. During those first 3–4 days, use a very thin layer of Aquaphor, or another ointment your artist approves. Too much ointment can suffocate the tattoo and cause seeping or heavy scabbing.
  4. Around day 5, switch to an unscented, dye-free lotion if it's dry or itchy. Keep that up for about 2 weeks as needed. The previous instructions named Lubriderm, Jergens, Cornhuskers, Curel, or Eucerin.
  5. Redness, irritation, itchiness, and swelling are common while it heals.
  6. Showers are fine. Try to keep body wash and shampoo off the fresh tattoo. If they hit it, wash the tattoo with Dial Gold again afterward.
  7. For the first 2–3 weeks, avoid any water that isn't a shower. No lakes, pools, hot tubs, oceans, or baths. Don't submerge the tattoo.
  8. Stay out of the sun. Sun on a healing tattoo can lead to infection and extra fading.
