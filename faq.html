Everything you need to know about walk-ins, consultations, pricing, Cherry Street parking, body piercings, and tattoo healing.

Booking & Walk-Ins

Do you take walk-in tattoos in downtown Macon?

Yes. Walk-ins are welcome Tuesday through Saturday whenever an artist chair is open. Smaller flash pieces, script, and straightforward designs are often taken the same day. For larger custom work, sleeves, or cover-ups that require custom drawing time, book a consultation or call (478) 259-1829 before heading down to 484 Cherry St.

What are Tooth & Nail Tattoo Parlour's hours?

Tooth & Nail is open five days a week at 484 Cherry St in downtown Macon, GA: Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday from 1:00 PM to 8:00 PM, and Friday and Saturday from 1:00 PM to 10:00 PM. The parlour is closed on Sunday and Monday.

Where are you located and where do I park on Cherry Street?

We are located at 484 Cherry St, Macon, GA 31201, right in the heart of downtown Macon near the Cherry Street dining and retail corridor and a short drive from Mercer University. Street parking is available along Cherry Street and adjacent downtown blocks.

Pricing & Policies

How much does a tattoo cost?

Pricing depends on final size, placement on the body, level of detail, color saturation, and artist time—so we do not quote flat prices blindly over social media or phone without reviewing your idea. Call (478) 259-1829, submit a consultation request online, or stop by the shop for an accurate estimate.

Do I need to put down a deposit to book a custom appointment?

Custom appointments and multi-hour sessions require a deposit confirmed directly with your chosen artist or the shop desk (for example, several resident artists take a $50 booking deposit applied toward the tattoo). Always confirm your artist's exact deposit policy and accepted payment method directly—never send money to unverified accounts.

What is the minimum age and ID requirement?

Plan on being 18 years of age or older with a valid, unexpired government-issued photo ID (such as a driver's license, state ID, or passport) for any tattoo or piercing service. If you have questions regarding identification or age rules, call (478) 259-1829 before arriving.

Healing & Aftercare

Does getting a tattoo or piercing hurt?

Yes—both tattooing and piercing involve discomfort, though tolerance varies widely by person and placement. Spots over bone or thin skin (ribs, feet, hands, sternum) feel sharper than fleshier areas like the outer arm or calf. Eat a solid meal beforehand, hydrate well, and ask your artist what to expect for your chosen placement.

Can I go swimming or take a bath with a new tattoo?

No. Do not submerge a fresh tattoo in pools, lakes, rivers, oceans, hot tubs, or bathtubs for at least 3 weeks while the skin seals and heals. Soaking softens healing skin and sharply raises infection risk. Brief, clean showers are fine—if you have a beach trip coming up, schedule your tattoo for after you get home.

Why does my new tattoo look like ink is leaking out under the wrap?

Your tattoo is not fading or rejecting ink. During the first few days, your body produces plasma and lymph fluid to heal the skin, which naturally mixes with excess surface pigment to form a dark, cloudy liquid under Soku or second-skin wraps. Follow the wash and moisturizing steps on our Aftercare page and check with your artist if anything looks unusual.
